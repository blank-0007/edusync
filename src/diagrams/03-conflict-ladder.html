EduSync · conflict model
The merge ladder: five levels, zero silent loss
Auto-merge when a rule can decide; freeze and escalate when it cannot. No level is permitted to discard a version — L4 exists to prove that "resolved" never means "one side was thrown away".

The guarantee
Lossless at every level — L4 is where “no silent loss” is made explicit
A merge that discards one side is data loss with extra steps. History is the guaranteed escape hatch: every L2–L4 decision writes a conflict row, and both sides stay in the op log forever.
