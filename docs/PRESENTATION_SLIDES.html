THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM
Why Traditional Educational Apps Break on Real Networks
REAL-WORLD STUDENT PAIN
🚌 The Bus Ride Reality
  • Commute Signal Drops: Students commuting on buses or trains face constant signal blackouts.
  • Fake Online Status: Browsers claim 'online' even behind dead captive portals with zero data flow.
  • Frozen Loading Spinners: Traditional school apps freeze for 30s timeouts, locking students out of their notes.
  • Blocked Homework Access: Students cannot open instructions or study rubrics once disconnected.
💥 Devastating Exam Wipeouts
  • The Disappearing Essay: A student writes 500 words, clicks Submit in a dead zone, and the page refreshes to blank.
  • Duplicate Submissions: Panicked students spam Submit 10 times, causing duplicate entries or spam blocks.
  • Silent Overwrites: Naive Last-Write-Wins wipes simultaneous offline edits with zero warning.
  • Student Anxiety: Fear of lost work causes immense stress during digital exam submissions.
💡 How EduSync Fixes This
  • Instant 0ms Local Save: Every keystroke commits immediately to phone storage before touching the wire.
  • Pre-Cached Courses: Textbooks and lesson videos open instantly with zero internet required.
  • Instant Confirmed Receipts: Students receive an official submission receipt right on their screen.
  • Gentle Wi-Fi Reconnect: Background delta queue delivers work automatically when signal returns.

TECHNOLOGY STACK
The Tech Stack: What We Used & Why (In Plain Words)
PURPOSE-BUILT ARCHITECTURE
📱
FRONTEND SCREEN
React 18 & Vite
What it is: The visual buttons and screens the student touches.
  • 0ms Response: Updates instantly as you type so screen never lags.
  • TypeScript Safety: Catches code bugs before reaching students.
  • Super-Light: Loads in <1s even on budget $50 smartphones.
💾
DEVICE STORAGE
IndexedDB & SW
What it is: A private, tamper-proof storage box right inside the browser.
  • Survives Crashes: Saved on phone disk; survives dead batteries.
  • Service Worker: Keeps textbooks offline so books open with 0 internet.
  • Immutable Log: Every keystroke recorded like a paper diary.
☁️
GLOBAL CLOUD
Cloudflare D1 & Fastify
What it is: The fast server and database deployed in 300+ cities globally.
  • 300+ Edge Nodes: Near students anywhere in the world for speed.
  • Duplicate Shield: Absorbs repeated clicks so students aren't double-submitted.
  • Incremental Sync: Only sends new words you typed, saving mobile data.
🛡️
QUALITY & AUDIT
Vitest & axe-core
What it is: Automated robot testers that verify the system never breaks.
  • 108 Automated Tests: Tests every network drop and crash scenario.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: Audited to ensure visually impaired students have access.
